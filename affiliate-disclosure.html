What Are Affiliate Links?

Some links on ToolCouponVault are affiliate links. When you click these links and make a purchase or sign up for a service, we may earn a small commission from the merchant at no additional cost to you.

How We Use Affiliate Revenue

Affiliate commissions help us maintain ToolCouponVault, pay for hosting, and continue providing free coupon codes to our users.

Editorial Independence

Our editorial process is independent from affiliate relationships. We feature coupons based on:

  • Value and discount percentage
  • Quality and reputation of the tool
  • User demand and popularity
  • Availability and validity of the coupon

We never promote a tool simply because it has affiliate potential. Our goal is to help you save money on tools you'll actually use.

Your Savings Are Real

The discounts you find on ToolCouponVault are the same discounts available elsewhere. Using our links doesn't increase your price — it may actually save you more because we often negotiate exclusive deals for our users.
