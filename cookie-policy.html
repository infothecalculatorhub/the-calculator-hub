Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on ToolCouponVault

Home/Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: We use essential cookies for theme preferences and cookie consent.

  • Theme Preference: Remembers dark/light mode selection (1 year, local storage)
  • Cookie Consent: Stores your cookie preference choice (1 year, local storage)

Third-Party Services

  • Tailwind CSS: For styling (CDN, no cookies)
  • Google Favicon Service: For displaying tool logos (no cookies)

Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google AdSense Cookies

  • Purpose: To personalize ads and measure ad effectiveness
  • Provider: Google (adservice.google.com, doubleclick.net)
  • Duration: Varies (typically session to 1 year)
  • Opt-out: You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings

We also use essential cookies to remember your theme preference (dark or light mode) and cookie consent preferences. These are stored locally in your browser.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of personalized advertising:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance

Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Check back here for the latest information.
